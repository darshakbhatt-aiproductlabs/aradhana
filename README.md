# Aradhana · आराधना

A pastel inner temple — for little hands and grown hearts.

Live: [darshakbhatt-aiproductlabs.github.io/aradhana](https://darshakbhatt-aiproductlabs.github.io/aradhana/)

## Three doors

| Practice | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Japa** | A soft mala. Tap a bead. Keep the Name. |
| **Vāṇī** | Vedic recitation on a cinematic divine screen — word by word, as if the deity is singing to you. |
| **Murti** | A living deity. Each form carries its mantra. A full manas puja of jal, milk, tilak, flowers, dhoop, aarti, and pranam. |

Manas puja lives inside **Murti** — offerings sit on the tray beneath the deity, in order, with a blessing at the end.

No account. Preferences stay on the device.

## Use it

1. From the courtyard, pick a door — or use the tabs.
2. **Vāṇī:** tap Play. Gayatri is ready. Open the library for more hymns, or paste your own.
3. **Murti:** choose a form (Shiva has many). Their mantra is ready. Offer, then bow.
