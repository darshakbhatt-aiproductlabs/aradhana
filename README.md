A pastel inner temple — for little hands and grown hearts.

Live: [darshakbhatt-aiproductlabs.github.io/aradhana](https://darshakbhatt-aiproductlabs.github.io/aradhana/)

## Three doors

| Practice | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Japa** | A soft mala. Tap a bead. Keep the Name. ← Setup and Home are always one tap away. |
| **Vāṇī** | A concert screen for recitation. Spotlight, karaoke, ticker, lower-third, mandala, caption, night, stage, temple TV. Dim the deity. Mirror or project. |
| **Murti** | A 3D bronze murti in a sanctum. Choose **Shiva** or the **Shivling**. Abhishekam pours from the crown: jal, milk, sandal, tripundra, bilva, dhoop, aarti, pranam. |

No account. Preferences stay on the device.

## Use it

1. From the courtyard, pick a door — or use Home / the tabs. Every practice has Home at the top. Tap an open tab again to return to its start.
2. **Vāṇī:** tap Play. Open Stage for lyric style, screen shape, backdrop opacity, glow, gold dust.
3. **Murti:** pick a form (Shiva and Shivling are separate). Drag to walk around. Pour from the crown — milk runs down the stone.
