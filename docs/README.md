A pastel inner temple — for little hands and grown hearts.

Live: [darshakbhatt-aiproductlabs.github.io/aradhana](https://darshakbhatt-aiproductlabs.github.io/aradhana/)

## Three doors

| Practice | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Japa** | A soft mala. Tap a bead. Keep the Name. ← Setup and Home are always one tap away. |
| **Vāṇī** | A concert screen for recitation. Spotlight, karaoke, ticker, lower-third, mandala, caption, night, stage, temple TV. Dim the deity. Mirror or project. |
| **Murti** | Your bronze 3D murtis — Ganesha to Shani — each with its mantra. Natural male or female chant. Jal runs from the crown. Tilak on the brow. Cinematic puja. Send more gods any time. |

No account. Preferences stay on the device.

## Use it

1. From the courtyard, pick a door — or use the tabs. The top-left arrow goes home. Tap an open tab again to return to its start.
2. **Vāṇī:** tap Play. Open Stage for lyric style, screen shape, backdrop opacity, glow, gold dust.
3. **Murti:** pick a murti. Pour from the crown. Switch Male / Female for a natural chant. Open cinematic for just the steps.
4. Tap the music mark to play any song from [Chitta Dhyana](https://youtube.com/@chittadhyana) behind Japa, Vāṇī, or Murti.
