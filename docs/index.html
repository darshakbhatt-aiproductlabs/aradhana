आराधना

Aradhana

A pastel inner temple — for little hands and grown hearts. Japa, Vāṇī, and a living murti.

01जप

Japa

Mala recitation

A soft mala. Tap a bead. Keep the Name, together.
02वाणी

Vāṇī

Divine screen

A concert screen for the Name — lyric cuts, frames, dim the backdrop, project it.
03मूर्ति

Murti

Living puja

Bronze murtis in the sanctum. Pour jal from the crown. Tilak on the brow.

Sit. Breathe. Begin.
