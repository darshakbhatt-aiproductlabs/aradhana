आराधना

Aradhana

The inner temple. Four doors into bhakti — japa, path, puja, and a murti that lives when you touch it.

01जप

Japa

Mala recitation

A child’s mala. Tap a bead. Keep the name.
02पाठ

Path

Follow the verse

Walk a hymn word by word, like a finger on the page.
03पूजा

Puja

Manas puja

Offer water, flowers, lamp — in the mind, to Shiva.
04मूर्ति

Murti

Living deity

Place a murti. Touch it. Let it wake.

Sit. Breathe. Begin.
